## Artwork

The hero uses `public/images/explorer.png`, generated with the built-in imagegen
tool. It is a transparent raster illustration, with gentle CSS movement. The
separate **Live 3D** view loads the actual interactive Poly astronaut model in
Three.js on demand. These are two different visual representations, not the
same model. Model attribution is in `public/models/ATTRIBUTION.md` and the footer.

### Final image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent-background hero artwork for a premium software engineer portfolio themed around space exploration.
Create a photorealistic high-end 3D cinematic render of a single astronaut in zero gravity, full body entirely visible, isolated on a genuinely transparent background with alpha. No text, typography, labels, logos, flag patches, watermarks, or surrounding scenery.
Astronaut wearing an intricately detailed off-white EVA spacesuit with realistic fabric folds, seam stitching, ribbed joints, metallic connectors, chest life-support controls and backpack. Large rounded helmet with glossy nearly-black smoked visor reflecting a narrow white softbox and very subtle chartreuse light. No visible face. A poised, natural floating pose angled diagonally about 25 degrees: helmet toward upper right, boots toward lower left; one knee casually bent and one arm extending forward toward the camera, other relaxed outward. Three-quarter front view. Both hands naturally formed gloved hands. Athletic realistic human proportions, not a toy or cartoon. Sculptural, editorial, striking silhouette. White fabric with graphite details and tiny muted lime accents. Soft white key light from upper left and a very subtle electric-lime rim on right, deep but readable shadows. Fine surface detail and sophisticated realistic materials. Sharp focus, no motion blur. High resolution portrait 4:5 composition with generous transparent margin all around. The astronaut should occupy roughly 85% of canvas height. This will overlap giant typography in a dark website hero.
